## Concepts

- **Tool Overview**: seqera (v1.2.0) is an AI-powered CLI assistant for bioinformatics workflows.
- **Core Function**: Provides AI assistance for workflow development and Seqera Platform.
- **Algorithm**: Leverages AI for workflow optimization and assistance.
- **Input/Output**: Accepts text queries and produces workflow recommendations.
- **AI Assistance**: Focuses on intelligent workflow development and optimization.
- **Applications**: Workflow development, optimization, and platform management.

## Pitfalls

- **AI Limitations**: AI suggestions may not always be optimal.
- **Platform Access**: Requires Seqera Platform access credentials.
- **Network Requirements**: Requires internet connectivity for AI services.
- **Cost Considerations**: Some AI features may have associated costs.
- **Version Compatibility**: Different versions may have breaking changes.
- **Documentation**: Some features have limited documentation.

## Examples

### Get help
**Args:** `seqera help`
**Explanation:** Shows available commands and options.

### Optimize workflow
**Args:** `seqera optimize workflow.nf`
**Explanation:** AI-powered workflow optimization.

### Generate workflow
**Args:** `seqera generate --template rna-seq`
**Explanation:** Generates workflow from template.

### Analyze workflow
**Args:** `seqera analyze workflow.nf`
**Explanation:** Analyzes workflow for improvements.

### Verbose logging
**Args:** `seqera optimize workflow.nf -v`
**Explanation:** `-v` enables verbose output for debugging.

### Version check
**Args:** `seqera --version`
**Explanation:** Shows current version.

### Configure
**Args:** `seqera configure`
**Explanation:** Configures Seqera AI assistant.
