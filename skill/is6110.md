## Concepts

- **Tool Overview**: is6110 - A bioinformatics tool for detecting IS6110 insertion sequence locations in Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex genomes
- **IS6110 Element**: A 1358-bp insertion sequence specific to MTBC, used as a gold standard for strain typing
- **Genotyping Applications**: Enables epidemiological tracking of TB transmission and strain identification
- **Copy Number Variation**: High copy number strains (10-15 copies) vs low copy number strains (1-5 copies) have different evolutionary histories
- **Primer Design**: Uses specific primers (IS7, IS9, IScom) flanking IS6110 elements for detection
- **NGS Integration**: Processes next-generation sequencing data to map insertion sites across the genome

## Pitfalls

- **MTBC Specific**: Only works with Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex; will not detect IS6110 in other organisms
- **Reference Dependence**: Accuracy depends on the quality of the reference genome used for alignment
- **Low Copy Number**: Strains with few IS6110 copies may provide insufficient discriminatory power
- **PCR Bias**: Traditional PCR-based methods may miss some insertion sites due to primer specificity
- **Mobile Element Variability**: IS6110 sequences can accumulate mutations (13.3% observed in some studies)
- **Assembly Quality**: Requires good quality NGS assemblies; fragmented contigs may miss insertions

## Examples

### Basic IS6110 detection
**Args:** `is6110 --input sample.bam --reference h37rv.fasta --output is6110_results.txt`
**Explanation:** Identifies IS6110 insertion sites from aligned BAM file against H37Rv reference genome.

### With variant calling
**Args:** `is6110 --input tb_sample.bam --reference h37rv.fasta --output results.vcf --call-variants`
**Explanation:** Detects insertion sites and identifies sequence variants within IS6110 elements.

### PCR primer design
**Args:** `is6110 --input assembly.fasta --design-primers --output primers.txt`
**Explanation:** Generates PCR primer pairs for validation of detected insertion sites.

### Multiple samples analysis
**Args:** `is6110 --input-dir ./bam_files/ --reference h37rv.fasta --output-dir ./results/`
**Explanation:** Processes multiple BAM files in batch mode for cohort-level analysis.

### Copy number estimation
**Args:** `is6110 --input sorted.bam --reference h37rv.fasta --estimate-copy-number`
**Explanation:** Quantifies IS6110 copy number and reports strain classification (high/low copy).

### Flanking sequence extraction
**Args:** `is6110 --input assembly.fasta --extract-flanking 500 --output flanking_sequences.fasta`
**Explanation:** Extracts 500bp sequences flanking each IS6110 insertion for phylogenetic analysis.
