## Concepts

- **Tool Overview**: X! Tandem - Protein identification tool.
- **Core Function**: Identifies proteins from mass spectrometry data.
- **Input**: Mass spectrometry data.
- **Output**: Identified proteins.
- **Installation**: Download from official site
- **Use Case**: Proteomics, bioinformatics.

## Pitfalls

- **Memory**: May require significant memory for large datasets.
- **Complexity**: May have steep learning curve.
